Child Safety Standards

Chill Letter has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualization of minors, sextortion, trafficking, or any behavior that exploits or endangers children.

Users are prohibited from creating, uploading, sending, requesting, or distributing any content involving CSAE or CSAM.

Chill Letter provides in-app reporting tools that allow users to report profiles, messages, letters, images, or behavior that may violate our child safety standards.

When we become aware of suspected CSAE or CSAM, we may remove the content, suspend or terminate the account, preserve relevant information where legally required, and report confirmed cases to the appropriate regional or national authorities.

Chill Letter complies with applicable child safety laws and cooperates with lawful requests from authorities.
